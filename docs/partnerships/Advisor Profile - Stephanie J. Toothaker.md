## 5. Fit against the repository's actual open legal questions

**Assessment.** On the face of the public positioning recorded in S9, the practice type matches the problem type: the South Andrews entitlement question is a characterization-and-pathway problem with a governmental-relations dimension, not a technical zoning-opinion problem. That is a statement about **category fit**, classified as a recommendation. It is not a conclusion about this attorney's suitability, which requires V-3 through V-7 and a project-specific conversation.

## 7. Recommended engagement posture

**Classification: recommendation. Not an owner decision and not authority to retain anyone.**

1. **Narrow, paid, deliverable-defined first engagement.** A bounded written land-use and entitlement strategy memorandum, not an open-ended entitlement mandate. S9 §10 proposes twelve scope items and a defined deliverable; that shape is sound and is consistent with the repository's own sequencing, which places the land-use counsel opinion at step 1.5 of Stage 1 — **before** any offer contingency expires and before the Zoning Verification Letter and pre-application meeting it feeds.
2. **Cost context.** The [Entitlement and Utility Roadmap](../technical/Entitlement%20and%20Utility%20Roadmap.md) carries a planning allowance of **$15,000–$30,000** for the land-use counsel opinion (step 1.5), inside a total pre-acquisition diligence program of roughly **$100,000–$200,000**. These are repository planning allowances, not quotations, and the actual fee is unknown (V-4).
3. **Sequence value.** One counsel opinion plus one pre-application meeting is capable of closing OQ-03, OQ-26, OQ-27, and OQ-30 together at no material incremental cost — the repository already records this as the most efficient available cluster of resolutions.
4. **Do not expand the first engagement** to partner identification, City advocacy, or application filing. Those require separate authorization, a resolved acquisition posture, and in the case of advocacy, a determination on lobbying registration (V-6).
