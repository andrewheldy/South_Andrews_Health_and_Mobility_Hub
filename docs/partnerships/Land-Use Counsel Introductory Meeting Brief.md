## 1. Purpose and limits

This brief exists so that a single meeting produces decision-grade answers rather than a general conversation. It does three things:

1. States the project position **as the repository actually records it today**, including the negative feasibility finding, so that nothing presented to counsel is stronger than the evidence behind it.
2. Isolates the questions where a land-use opinion changes what the owner does next, and grounds each one in the specific repository conflict it would resolve.
3. Records what must not be represented, and the material conflicts that must be disclosed rather than smoothed over.

Two things this brief deliberately does **not** do. It does not adopt the revised acquisition basis described in §5, and it does not treat any scheme as an authorized development program.

### 2.5 Mobility, stated accurately

Gross stalls are not fleet capacity. After the building's own code-required parking, secure-zone boundary loss, turnaround, and maneuvering reserve, the maximum-fleet scheme yields roughly **140 operational fleet positions, not 300–400** — at approximately **$545,000 per usable position** on land priced around $209/SF. The leading balanced scheme yields **18 ground bays and no structured surplus at all**. Moving depot functions off site cuts the required electrical service from about **3,208 kW to 1,143 kW**, avoiding medium-voltage service and roughly **$3M of capital**. No autonomous-vehicle, fleet, or charging revenue is underwritten in any base case (CAN-012), and no fleet operator, AV company, or mobility partner has any documented relationship with this project.

## 7. Testing the proposed contract periods against our own planning ranges

2. **What exact governmental milestone should protect the buyer before closing**, and how is "acceptable" defined so that a conditioned approval is not a trap?
3. **How long must the feasibility and entitlement periods actually be**, given §7?
4. **How should the mobility functions be structured and described** so that they survive review without either overstating them or forfeiting future optionality?
5. **What team, budget, and sequence are required to reach a defensible go/no-go decision**, and in what order?

The purpose is not validation of the concept. It is a candid assessment of whether it can be approved, how it must change, what pursuing it costs, and how the sponsor should be protected contractually while finding out.

## 10. After the meeting

1. Record counsel's answers as **advice received from a prospective advisor**, not as determinations. A verbal opinion does not close a gate; G2 requires a written City determination and G3 requires a written counsel opinion.
2. Where an answer contradicts a repository position, add it to the [Contradiction and Validation Register](../planning/Contradiction%20and%20Validation%20Register.md) rather than overwriting the position.
3. Update [Open Questions](../planning/Open%20Questions.md) statuses; close nothing without the written instrument the register names.
4. Any engagement, fee arrangement, or scope agreement requires a new owner decision record and belongs in [`docs/decisions/`](../decisions/), with the paper filed to `sources/raw/`.
5. Add any offer, letter of intent, rider, or correspondence to `sources/raw/` as generated (MA-18) and register it.
