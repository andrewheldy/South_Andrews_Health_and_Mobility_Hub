## 1. The determination required before anything is sent

S9 §12 lists twenty-two items to send before the meeting. Before any of them moves, one question must be answered by the owner, because the repository's controls turn on it:

**Is transmission to a prospective, unengaged attorney "external use" under `CLAIMS_AND_EVIDENCE_POLICY.md`?**

| Reading | Consequence |
|---|---|
| **It is external use** | Every item is **blocked**. [Document Update Order](../planning/Document%20Update%20Order.md) Step 8 gates external material behind model-vNext, ODP v2, and an independent audit — none of which has occurred. The [External Publication Checklist](../external/EXTERNAL_PUBLICATION_CHECKLIST.md) returns `blocked` on multiple items today |
| **It is confidential professional-advisor disclosure, not publication** | Material may be shared under an explicit confidentiality and status cover, provided the claims policy's substantive rules are still honored: accurate partner status, no implied approvals, and the current negative feasibility finding neither omitted nor obscured |

**Recommendation (classification: recommendation, not a decision).** Treat it as the second reading, and record the determination as an owner decision. The rationale is that the external-publication gate exists to prevent overstated marketing claims reaching the market, while the purpose here is the opposite — obtaining candid adverse legal advice, which requires disclosing the weaknesses in full. But the two readings produce different actions, so the owner should rule rather than let a session infer it. Escalated as **OQ-35**.

Whichever reading is adopted, the substantive rules do not relax. Anything sent must carry the cover disclosures in §5.

## 5. Required cover disclosures on anything transmitted

Every item sent must carry, in the covering communication and not only inside the attachments:

1. **Prospective acquisition. The sponsor does not currently own the property.** No offer, letter of intent, term sheet, option, or contract exists.
2. No entitlement, City determination, City contact, utility capacity, environmental clearance, updated survey, title work, financing, or contractor pricing exists.
3. No health system, developer, operator, tenant, government body, airport, port, utility, lender, or investor is committed, involved, or endorsing. Every organization named is prospective.
4. No scheme is an adopted development program. Test fits, scenarios, and massing studies are planning-level and unapproved; **SA-C is a preliminary reference test fit**.
5. **Both current financial models indicate the base project as modeled does not satisfy the modeled institutional return requirements, and the shortfall is not curable by the land price.** This finding must not be omitted or minimized.
6. All costs are AACE Class 5 planning estimates (−30%/+50%). All rents, absorption, demand, and return figures are unvalidated planning inputs or model outputs.
7. Third-party documents are sent within their own scope: the appraisal is a seller/lender-side opinion with documented internal inconsistencies; the Native Realty study was prepared for a car-rental-operator client thesis and its program is a rejected scenario.
8. The acquisition basis is a **ratified but nonbinding tactical negotiation figure of $7,500,000** (D-P13) — not an economically supported land value and not authority to submit an offer. The **modeled** land basis behind every figure in the material is **$8,000,000**, unchanged; the two are never blended. No offer exists or is authorised, and price limits and structure remain undecided.
9. Confidentiality expectation stated expressly, and the request that no filing, City contact, or third-party communication occur without written instruction.
