## Governing conclusion that travels with every disclosure

> **The tested program does not support the land at any price, including zero.**

This finding may not be omitted, minimized, softened, or deferred in any material sent at any stage. Withholding it would breach `CLAIMS_AND_EVIDENCE_POLICY.md` and would also produce worse legal advice, because it changes what contract protection is worth buying.

## Stage definitions

| Stage | Trigger | Character of disclosure |
|---|---|---|
| **Conflict check** | D-P15 ratified; no clearance yet | **Identity-level only.** Parties, property, parcels, counterparties. No analysis, no figures, no registers, no documents |
| **Initial consultation** | Written conflicts clearance received; B-1/B-2 filled; scope confirmed | Substantive material needed to answer the land-use questions. **Privilege may not attach before an engagement letter is executed** (D-P15 §2.3) |
| **After engagement** | Engagement letter executed | Full working record within the engaged scope |
| **Do not disclose yet** | — | Withheld at all three stages pending a separate ruling or a document that does not yet exist |

Every transmission at any stage carries the nine cover disclosures in [Counsel Meeting Materials Register](Counsel%20Meeting%20Materials%20Register.md) §5 and is entered in its transmission log.

## Standing rules

1. **Identity-level at the conflicts stage; documents only after clearance.** No exception for convenience or momentum.
2. **A "Yes" is permission, not an instruction.** Send the least that answers the question.
3. **No item may be sent without the nine cover disclosures**, including the negative feasibility finding and the prospective-acquisition status.
4. **Log every transmission** — date, recipient, item, version or hash, authorizing instruction — in [Counsel Meeting Materials Register](Counsel%20Meeting%20Materials%20Register.md) §6.
5. **Never send an ungoverned file** as a project document.
6. **Never characterize a negotiation figure as value**, a ceiling, a walk-away price, an approved offer, or transaction authority.
7. **Advice received is not a repository fact.** Contradictions go to the [Contradiction and Validation Register](../planning/Contradiction%20and%20Validation%20Register.md); gates close only on the written instruments they name.
8. **Re-clearance is required** before any specific development partner, health system, or operator is named to counsel.
