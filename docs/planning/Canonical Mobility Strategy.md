# Canonical Mobility Strategy

**Status:** Draft for owner review · **Date:** 2026-07-26 · **Author:** AI planning session
**Governing documents:** `AGENTS.md` (mobility = enabling infrastructure; no speculative AV revenue), `PROJECT_GLOSSARY.md` (EV-ready vs EV-installed; AV staging definitions)
**Related:** [Canonical Infrastructure Strategy](Canonical%20Infrastructure%20Strategy.md) · [Current Development Program](Current%20Development%20Program.md) §3 · [Master Assumption Register](Master%20Assumption%20Register.md) §11

> **Standing rules (governance, non-negotiable):** Mobility systems **enable** the healthcare anchor; they are not the thesis. "Mobility-enabled" does not imply AV deployment, partner commitment, or revenue. No AV or edge revenue is underwritten in any base case. Every fleet operator named below is market context only.

## 1. Parking (the load-bearing mobility layer)

- **Role:** monetizable corridor deficit today; satisfies the 4.0–5.0/1,000 SF clinical ratio; funds early cash flow; convertible for future re-use. *(ODP §2.1; thesis SD/REC.)*
- **Operating model (adopted):** shared parking — medical by day, public/commuter off-peak; gateless LPR operations for low staffing; patient/visitor priority nearest the clinical elevator. *(ODP Ch.13H; S3 stack notes.)*
- **Counts:** basis-dependent (340/440 Basis A vs 231/308 + 18 AV bays Basis B) — unresolved with the program (OQ-14).
- **Revenue:** dual constructs ($2,400/space/yr blended vs $275/mo × 55% monetized) — unresolved (MB-09); the underlying "Site Analysis pricing survey" is not in the repo (MA-16).
- **Convertibility:** flat plates/removable ramps/12-ft F2F so parking levels can become clinical/office/other occupied use if vehicle demand falls — the hedge against AV-driven parking erosion (ODP R-7).

## 2. EV charging

- **Adopted rule:** conduit and panel capacity to **100% of stalls** during construction; energize a subset day-one (~40 chargers per owner directive; ODP frames 10–20% energized).
- **Day-one mix — UNRESOLVED (OQ-21):** 32 L2 + 8 DCFC installed (S2) vs 40 L2 installed + 4 DCFC stub-outs with hardware deferred until an operator commitment (S3). S3's demand-gated DCFC treatment aligns better with the phase-innovation rule; owner to decide with the FPL study.
- **Economics (adopted working value):** ~$120K/yr net charging income across ~40 units, utilization-sensitive; managed charging holds the 40-port peak near 192 kW.
- **EV-ready totals (working):** 80 ports (6-story) / 120 (8-story) of rough-in beyond installed units; "EV-ready" ≠ chargers installed ≠ utility capacity confirmed (glossary discipline).
- **No §30C credit underwritten** (deadline 6/30/2026; pending counsel).

## 3. AV staging & fleet readiness

- **What is in scope now:** a separated, secured ground-level staging capability with independent ingress/egress (12,000 SF zone per S2 / 18 bays per S3 — representation unresolved with the program basis), structural and power readiness, and access-control provisions. *(Owner directive; capability, not operation.)*
- **What is not:** any AV service, approval, demand, partnership, or revenue. **Base-case AV revenue = $0 everywhere.** S2's toggle-OFF is the compliant pattern; S3's inclusion of $194.4K/yr AV-bay revenue in base NOI is flagged for correction in the next model cycle (OQ-22).
- **Upside if a fleet operator signs (FORECAST only):** ~$300K/yr (S2: $25/SF/yr × 12,000 SF) or ~$194K/yr (S3: 18 bays × $900/mo) — quote only with "potential partner / subject to executed lease" language.
- **Operator landscape (context only — S3 `Sources!A14–A17` caveats adopted verbatim):** Waymo active Miami/Orlando (supports outreach relevance, **not site demand**); Zoox Miami testing/deployment; Tesla Robotaxi Miami operations; Freebee regional electric microtransit. "No partnership or Fort Lauderdale commitment assumed" — all four.
- **Activation trigger discipline:** each Phase-3+ activation (DCFC expansion, AV build-out, conversion) needs an explicit, owner-approved demand/partner trigger encoded in the financial model (ODP Ch.9; to be defined during model reconciliation).

## 4. Site circulation (adopted design intent, subject to traffic study OQ-05)

Andrews Ave = pedestrian/retail frontage, never the fleet throat · separated AV ingress and egress on secondary street edges · AV loop isolated from public/patient circulation before the first decision point · independent gates/queuing/wash/light-service in the AV zone · dedicated ride-share & patient drop-off · loading screened at rear · micro-mobility connections toward rail. *(S3 `P&M!A33–A37`, `E&M!A24–A28`; ODP Ch.6/13F.)*

## 5. Transit & public alignment

Between Brightline (1.8 mi) and the planned BCR South commuter station; FLL 2.8 mi; City/MPO mobility-hub goals open P3/MPO grant and TIF avenues — pursued as upside, **never load-bearing for feasibility** (ODP §11.2). A modest public plaza aligns with City public-realm goals.

## 6. What would change this strategy

A signed AV/charging master lease, minimum-use guarantee, or capital contribution would (a) flip the relevant toggle in the models, (b) close part of the modeled yield gap, and (c) require a logged owner decision elevating that element from optionality — the only sanctioned path by which mobility economics enter the base case.
