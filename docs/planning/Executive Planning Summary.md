## 2. The canonical picture (full statement: [Project Canon](Project%20Canon.md))

**South Andrews Healthcare and Mobility Hub** — a proposed healthcare-anchored development on the 0.88-acre, 38,207 SF corner assemblage at 901–917 S Andrews Avenue (prospective acquisition; sponsor does not own the property; working opening offer $8.0M vs an $11.1M seller-side appraisal with documented comp weaknesses). Healthcare (flexible clinical/medical co-working + med-tail) is the anchor; convertible structured parking, EV readiness, AV-staging capability, solar/battery, and a small data room are enabling infrastructure. The 6-story and 8-story schemes are both **scenarios** — neither adopted. Rejected/historical concepts (seller's "Agora" retail center, car-rental fleet hub, "Mobility Nexus" as primary thesis, residential/hotel schemes) are preserved as history, not deleted.

## 3. The finding the owner must see first

**Both current models independently conclude the base program does not pencil.** Yield on cost is ~3.8–4.1% against a 6.25–7.0% requirement: the 6v8 model shows development profit of **−$27.7M (6-story) / −$33.9M (8-story)** and ~1.2–1.9% ten-year unlevered IRRs; the 8M feasibility model expresses the same gap as **$1.9–3.2M per year of required partner/grant/master-lease support**. Neither model underwrites AV revenue (correctly, per governance). The critical path is therefore **evidence and partners — rent validation, a co-working/health-system anchor, grants, and land-basis discipline — not design spend.** (Model output, convergent; S2 `Returns`, S3 `Financing & Returns`.)
