# IIM Architecture and Writing Plan

**Status:** current · **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-26 · **External-use status:** internal_only
**Related:** [IIM Source Map](IIM%20Source%20Map.md) · [Document Update Order](Document%20Update%20Order.md) · [Institutional Investment Memorandum](../investment/Institutional%20Investment%20Memorandum.md)

This plan defines the architecture, audience treatment, and drafting rules for the [Institutional Investment Memorandum](../investment/Institutional%20Investment%20Memorandum.md) (IIM). Length is determined by evidence and decision needs, not a page target.

## 1. What the IIM is and is not

**Is:** the canonical *internal* investment-committee decision paper — a disciplined synthesis of what the opportunity is, what is verified, what is assumed or unresolved, why the project may merit continued investment, what could make it fail, what evidence is still required, and what decisions ownership must make.

**Is not:** the external Prospectus vNext ([Document Update Order](Document%20Update%20Order.md) Step 8, blocked pending model vNext, ODP v2, and independent audit); a market document; a source of newly ratified truth; or authority for any transaction, design, or expenditure. Where the IIM conflicts with the [Project Canon](Project%20Canon.md), decision records, governed registers, protected sources, or validated models, the higher-authority repository source controls.

**Authority basis for creating it now:** owner instruction of 2026-07-26 commissioning the IIM as internal decision-support material, plus owner review amendments A1–A7 of the same date. The document is `internal_only` and `draft`; its existence does not advance the Document Update Order.

## 3. Writing standard

Per `skills/institutional-prospectus/SKILL.md` (applied here to an internal document), `docs/governance/CLAIMS_AND_EVIDENCE_POLICY.md`, and CLAUDE.md rule 8:

1. Direct, evidence-led, candid prose; no hype, superlatives, or implied certainty. The prohibited-phrase list in the owner's IIM commission (e.g., "guaranteed demand", "perfectly positioned", "massive opportunity", "generational asset") is enforced by validation.
2. Every material claim carries an inline evidence label — **[VF]** verified fact · **[SD]** source-derived, not independently verified · **[WA]** working assumption · **[MO]** model output · **[REC]** recommendation · **[OQ]** open question · **[SUP]** superseded · **[REJ]** rejected scenario — plus a locator (`S# (sheet!cell)`, `S# p.N`, CAN/decision/register ID). Missing-document citations use "as cited by Sx".
3. Approved status vocabulary only (proposed / prospective / planning-level / subject to … / potential partner / working assumption / illustrative / not yet validated). A caveat elsewhere does not cure a misleading headline or table.
4. Key-metric tables carry a status column using: verified · source-reported · model-derived · owner input · working assumption · scenario · unresolved · superseded.
5. Dual-basis discipline: Basis A (S2) and Basis B (S3) never mixed or averaged; every sized figure names its basis.
6. The $8,000,000 figure follows owner amendment A2: each substantive occurrence either sits within the full D-P1 qualification or carries an adjacent D-P1 reference plus an approved label; tables never show the figure without an adjacent qualifier.
7. Recommendation/authorization separation per amendment A5: no action is described as approved, commissioned, funded, or authorized absent a ratified decision or later owner instruction.
8. No renderings exist; if any are added later they are labeled **Illustrative rendering** at point of use.

## 5. Validation plan for the IIM

Narrow extension of `scripts/validate_repository.py` (existing 103 checks unchanged in substance):

1. The IIM set (this plan, the [IIM Source Map](IIM%20Source%20Map.md), the IIM, and the `docs/investment/` index) joins the authoritative-surface language scans (ownership language, missing-governance references, historical names).
2. New IIM-specific checks: files exist with metadata and `internal_only` status; canonical name and canonical address present in the IIM; the feasibility-finding sentence present; per-occurrence $8M qualifier discipline (amendment A2), including rejection of formulations characterizing the figure as appraised/market value, valuation conclusion, approved offer, transaction authority, ceiling, maximum, or walk-away price; prohibited promotional phrases absent; no self-designation as `external_eligible`.
3. Existing repo-wide checks (internal links, source SHA-256 integrity, manifest invariants including `base_case_feasible: false`) already sweep the new files.

Run: `python3 scripts/validate_repository.py` from the repository root; requirement is 0 errors / 0 warnings. Workbook figures quoted in the IIM are re-verified read-only via `python3 scripts/verify_workbook_claims.py`.
