# Governed Project Workflow

Complete phases in order. A later phase may reveal a need to return to an earlier phase, but it may not bypass its controls.

## 1. Source organization

- Inventory files without changing their content.
- Classify each source as current, reference, legacy, or raw.
- Record filename, format, date, provenance, authority, scope, and integrity information.
- Preserve originals; do not move or rename them without explicit owner instruction.

**Output:** source inventory and identified organization issues.

## 2. Planning

- Read the governance set and applicable skills.
- Extract claims faithfully and inspect every relevant workbook sheet and formula.
- Build conflict, assumption, open-question, and evidence tables.
- Reconcile only what the source hierarchy and scope justify.
- Define the proposed work, validation plan, disclosures, and acceptance criteria.
- Do not implement a frontend during this phase.

**Output:** owner-reviewable plan with material conflicts clearly exposed.

## 3. Owner review

- Present adopted values, unresolved conflicts, recommendations, alternatives, and consequences.
- Obtain explicit decisions for material program, scope, naming, financial, partnership, or status questions.
- Record the decision-maker, date, decision, and scope.

**Gate:** no implementation based on unresolved material conflicts.

Owner ratifications dated 2026-07-26 authorize the constitutional and repository-refactor Phase 1 implementation recorded in `docs/decisions/`. They do not authorize resolution of the pending program, rent, tax, return-hurdle, land-ceiling, partner, utility, or entitlement questions.

## 4. Implementation

- Implement only the owner-reviewed scope.
- Verify material facts against original sources.
- Keep source-derived work separate from originals.
- Apply citations, evidence classifications, caveats, and status language.
- Maintain consistency across narrative, model outputs, visuals, captions, and interfaces.

**Output:** reviewable implementation and evidence trail.

## 5. Independent audit

- Review source fidelity, model logic, calculations, claims, citations, caveats, status, and downstream consistency independently from the implementer.
- Record findings by severity and external-use impact.

**Output:** audit report with pass, correction, or escalation decisions.

## 6. Corrections

- Resolve audit findings without obscuring prior assumptions or decisions.
- Revalidate affected calculations, claims, citations, visuals, and interfaces.
- Obtain owner review again when a correction changes a material decision or external representation.

**Gate:** external use only after material findings are resolved or expressly accepted with appropriate disclosure.

## 7. Design Intelligence connection

- Test connections between governed evidence and the actual design and communications surface.
- Confirm that structured claims, citations, status labels, renderings, and updates propagate correctly.
- Re-run relevant audits after connection.

The Design Intelligence connection test happens only after the repository has a real design and communications surface. It is not part of initial governance, source organization, or planning.
