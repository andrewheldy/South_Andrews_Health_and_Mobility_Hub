# Project Glossary

Older project names are historical only. They must not replace the current project name or imply that an earlier program remains active.

## Defined terms

**South Andrews Healthcare and Mobility Hub**  
The current project name. A proposed healthcare-anchored development whose mobility, parking, charging, energy, and operational systems serve as enabling infrastructure and differentiators.

**South Andrews Clinical & Mobility Center**  
A historical project name. Use only when accurately identifying an older source or concept.

**South Andrews Mobility Hub**  
A historical project name. Use only when accurately identifying an older source or concept.

**healthcare anchor**  
The clinical and healthcare program that establishes the project's primary purpose, demand logic, and institutional identity.

**mobility-enabled**  
Designed so parking, EV charging, AV readiness, access, circulation, and mobility operations support the healthcare anchor. The term does not imply autonomous-vehicle deployment, partner commitment, or revenue.

**structured parking**  
Parking accommodated in a purpose-built multilevel structure rather than solely in surface spaces.

**medical retail**  
Patient-facing retail or service uses related to health, wellness, pharmacy, diagnostics, durable medical equipment, or other healthcare needs.

**med-tail**  
Informal shorthand for medical retail. Prefer “medical retail” in formal external materials unless the audience understands the term.

**clinical space**  
Space intended for direct patient care, diagnosis, treatment, testing, or related clinical operations. It does not imply a licensed or committed operator.

**medical office**  
Office space intended for healthcare professionals or healthcare administration, which may or may not include direct clinical functions.

**EV-ready**  
Designed with identified capacity, pathways, space, or infrastructure to support future EV charging. It does not mean chargers are installed or utility capacity is confirmed.

**EV-installed**  
Charging equipment is installed and available or planned under a specifically documented scope. State quantity, type, operating status, and utility dependencies.

**AV staging**  
Space or operational capacity proposed for autonomous-vehicle arrival, departure, waiting, loading, or coordination. It does not imply AV service, approval, demand, partnership, or revenue.

**planning-level estimate**  
An early estimate prepared for evaluation and decision-making, subject to design development, diligence, market testing, and confirmation. It is not a bid, guarantee, or finalized cost.

**illustrative rendering**  
A visual representation of a possible design or experience, not an approved architectural design, entitlement, construction document, or promise of final appearance.

**prospective partner**  
An organization that could participate or has been approached or discussed but has no documented binding commitment. Name it as a potential partner unless documentation supports a stronger status.

**working assumption**  
An active planning input used pending validation, reconciliation, or owner approval.

**canonical repository**  
The owner-designated repository that contains the controlling governance, source organization, derived work, and implementation record for this project.

**property status**  
**Prospective acquisition. The sponsor does not currently own the property.**

**six-story scenario**  
A modeled or studied six-story development alternative. It is not the approved program unless adopted through an explicit current owner decision.

**eight-story scenario**  
A modeled or studied eight-story development alternative. It is not the approved program unless adopted through an explicit current owner decision.
