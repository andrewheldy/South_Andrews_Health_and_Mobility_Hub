# Claims and Evidence Policy

Apply this policy to every material external-facing claim in narrative, tables, models, captions, presentations, renderings, metadata, and digital interfaces.

## Required claim record

For each material claim, record:

- **Supporting source** — exact source, location, date, and relevant page, section, sheet, range, or formula
- **Authority classification** — the source tier and applicable evidence classification from `AGENTS.md`
- **Confidence level** — high, medium, or low, with a brief basis
- **Required caveat** — qualifying language necessary to prevent overstatement
- **External-use status** — approved, approved with caveat, internal only, or blocked pending resolution

No claim is cleared for external use solely because it appears in a model, appraisal, prior prospectus, rendering, or owner working file.

## Prohibited unsupported claims

Do not make unsupported claims of:

- property ownership
- approved entitlement
- committed healthcare partners
- committed mobility partners
- guaranteed hospital spillover
- guaranteed parking demand
- finalized construction cost
- finalized utility capacity
- approved architectural design
- quantified economic impact without analysis
- City endorsement
- unsupported returns

Also do not imply these claims through logos, adjacency, captions, passive voice, visual treatment, or omission of caveats.

## Approved status language

Use the following language when accurate:

- proposed
- prospective
- planning-level
- subject to diligence
- subject to test fit
- subject to City review
- subject to utility confirmation
- potential partner
- working assumption
- illustrative
- not yet validated

Status language must be specific to the claim. A caveat elsewhere in a document does not cure a misleading headline, table, rendering, or callout.

## Review rule

Block external use when a material claim lacks a traceable source, has a material unresolved conflict, exceeds the source's scope, or cannot be stated with an adequate caveat. Preserve the claim in the working record with the appropriate evidence classification rather than deleting its history.
