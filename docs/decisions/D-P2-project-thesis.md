# D-P2 — Project thesis

**Decision.** The South Andrews Healthcare and Mobility Hub shall be developed and evaluated as a healthcare-anchored, mixed-use institutional project. Structured parking, EV charging, AV staging, solar, battery storage, data infrastructure, and related mobility systems are enabling infrastructure and optional value layers. The project shall not depend on speculative AV, charging, fleet-operating, edge-compute, or similar optional revenue to establish base-case feasibility.

**Treatment:** Healthcare is the anchor. Mobility, energy, charging, data, and AV systems are enabling or optional. Historical mobility-led, car-rental-led, residential-led, hotel-led, and seller Agora concepts remain preserved as rejected or historical scenarios.

**Dependency/caveat:** The strategy remains subject to test-fit, entitlement, engineering, market validation, operator input, and economics. Ratification is not proof of demand, feasibility, partner support, or approval.

**Affected:** canon; scenario register; financial controls; all downstream narratives and models.

**Supersedes:** Any active use of a mobility-led thesis or governance-clean base case containing speculative optional revenue.

**Review trigger:** Owner thesis change or proposed contractually supported optional revenue.
