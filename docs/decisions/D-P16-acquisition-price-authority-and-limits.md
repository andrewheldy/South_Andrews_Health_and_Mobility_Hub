# D-P16 — Acquisition price authority and limits

> **PROPOSED — NOT RATIFIED. THIS RECORD IS NOT AUTHORITY.**
> Nothing in this draft authorizes any offer, counteroffer, negotiation, deposit, or acquisition. Per [Decision Log](../planning/Decision%20Log.md) register discipline rule 2, a pending decision may never be cited as authority. **Three clauses below are deliberately blank and require an owner ruling.** No maximum price and no walk-away price has been selected, inferred, or recommended as a number in this record.

## 1. The governing conclusion this decision does not disturb

> **The tested program does not support the land at any price, including zero.**

This is the controlling economic finding (CVR-50; R-21; Project Canon §3, CAN-020/021/022). At a land basis of **$0**, no tested scheme exceeds **4.49%** yield on cost against a 7.0% planning hurdle; the break-even office rent on the leading scheme is approximately **$131/RSF** against $50 modeled; and the maximum land price the program as modeled can support is approximately **$0–$2M**.

**No price authority granted under this decision converts that conclusion into a supportable acquisition.** Any authorized maximum price is a decision to accept a known negative-residual position for strategic reasons the owner states, not a finding that the price is supported. That distinction must appear wherever a price figure appears.

## 3. Proposed decision

### 3.1 The $7.5M opening basis is not a maximum price

**D-P13's $7,500,000 is the nonbinding working opening-offer basis: a tactical negotiation figure.** It is not an economically supported land value, not proof of value, not an appraised value, not a price ceiling, not a walk-away price, and not authority to submit an offer.

An **authorized maximum purchase price is a different instrument entirely** — it is the ceiling on what the sponsor may agree to pay. It does not exist today and is proposed as **B-1**.

| Figure | Value | What it is | What it is not |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opening negotiation basis | **$7,500,000** (D-P13) | Where negotiation opens | Not a ceiling, not value, not authority |
| Maximum authorized price | **[OWNER BLANK — B-1]** | The ceiling on agreement | Does not exist today |
| Walk-away price | **[OWNER BLANK — B-2]** | The exit trigger | Does not exist today |
| Modeled land basis | **$8,000,000** (CAN-004a) | The input behind every model output; unchanged and not restated | Not the offer basis |
| Program-supported residual | **approximately $0–$2M** | Planning-level model output | Not a negotiating position; not what the land is worth to another buyer |

Context preserved, not adopted: the appraisal opinion is $11,100,000 with documented internal inconsistencies (CVR-51, CVR-52, CVR-53); the prior listing was $12,000,000; the buyer-side broker's stated "defensible range" is $7.65M–$9.0M, placing the opening basis marginally below its own floor. **None of these third-party ranges is an adopted limit.**

### 3.2 No offer or acquisition without authority

No offer, letter of intent, term sheet, rider, or purchase agreement may be prepared, signed, delivered, or verbally communicated at any figure until **B-1** is filled and the instrument itself is separately approved under clause 3.6.

### 3.3 Counteroffer authority

**Proposed default: no.** No counteroffer, price movement, or acceptance may be made without further owner approval, in writing, on each occasion — unless the owner pre-authorizes a band under **B-3**.

### 3.4 Walk-away price

**The sponsor has no current walk-away price.** D-P1 expressly left it unresolved and D-P13 did not supply one. Until **B-2** is filled, no figure may be represented internally or externally as a walk-away price, and none may be inferred from the opening basis, the appraisal, the broker's range, or the residual analysis.

### 3.5 Price remains contingent

Any price authority granted remains expressly contingent on the following findings, none of which exists today. All twelve decision gates in [Integrated Development Recommendation](../investment/Integrated%20Development%20Recommendation.md) §6 are open.

| Contingency | Gate | Status | What could move the price |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Environmental** | G1 | Open — hard stop. A Phase I **and a Phase II** ESA are listed among documents the appraiser reviewed; **neither is in the sponsor's possession** and no findings are recorded anywhere (MA-13 / OQ-28 / R-24) | Remediation exposure against a $250,000 placeholder allowance |
| **Entitlement — City use confirmation** | G2 | Open. Sources conflict and the controlling height text is truncated mid-word in the source PDF (OQ-03) | A narrower determination than assumed reduces developable value |
| **Entitlement — accessory parking** | G3 | Open. No source cites any ratio limit (OQ-27 / CVR-32) | An adverse reading removes the parking argument entirely |
| **Site plan** | G2 / G4 | Open. Approval above six storeys is discretionary; conditions may be costly | Conditions with material cost |
| **Test fit** | G4 | Open. Two irreconcilable program bases remain live (OQ-14 / D-P5) | Fewer stalls or less area than either model assumes |
| **Utility — FPL capacity** | G5 | Open. A single line spans $750K–$5.0M with no engineered basis (OQ-25) | Contribution-in-aid-of-construction cost |
| **Development partner** | G10 | Open. **No LOI, term sheet, or agreement exists with any party** | A partner contribution is the single most powerful lever identified |
| **Financing** | G11 | Open. A lender will underwrite the returns in the current package | No debt at these yields |
| **Market / rents** | G6 | Open. Break-even is ~$131/RSF against $50 modeled (OQ-15 / MB-07) | Validated rents materially above modeled |
| **Title and survey** | — | Open. The survey of record is dated 2015 and is not in the sponsor's possession (MA-10); "no known adverse encumbrances" is an appraisal assumption, not title work | Undisclosed easements |
| **Depot site** | G9 | Open. No off-site depot identified, priced, zoned-verified, or optioned | Strands mobility-specific capital |

If any contingency resolves adversely, **price authority lapses** and returns to the owner rather than adjusting automatically.

### 3.6 No purchase agreement without separate approval

**No purchase agreement, contract, or binding instrument may be signed without a separate, express owner decision at the time of signature.** Filling B-1 grants a price ceiling; it does not grant signature authority. Deposit structure, refundability, feasibility and entitlement periods, closing milestone, and seller covenants each require approval, informed by the corrected duration ranges sought under D-P15 §2.6(5) — the repository's own ranges suggest the proposed 75-day feasibility and 240-day entitlement periods are optimistic (OQ-32 / R-27).

### 3.7 No negotiation figure represents supported value

No figure used in negotiation — $7,500,000, any B-1 maximum, any counteroffer, or any figure the seller proposes — represents economically supported residual land value. **The residual land value under every tested program is materially negative** at institutional yields, ranging to −$32.2M for the largest scheme at a 7% yield on cost. Any document, model, communication, or representation that presents a negotiation figure as a supported or appraised value is prohibited.

## 4. What this decision does not resolve

Applicant and sponsor entity identity (OQ-33); transaction structure; the underwriting standard (MA-06 / OQ-18); the appraisal reconciliation memo (MB-19); and every diligence gate in §3.5. Counsel engagement and disclosure authority sit in **D-P15**, also pending.

## 5. Review trigger

Any owner ruling on B-1, B-2, or B-3; completion of any gate in §3.5; a seller counterproposal; model-vNext restatement; or an owner decision on transaction structure.
