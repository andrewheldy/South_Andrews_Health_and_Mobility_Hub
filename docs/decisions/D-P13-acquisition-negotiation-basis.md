# D-P13 — Acquisition negotiation basis

**Decision.** The current **nonbinding working opening-offer basis is $7,500,000**.

It is a **tactical negotiation figure**. It is **not** an economically supported land value, not proof of value, not an appraised value, not a price ceiling, not a walk-away price, and not, by itself, authority to submit an offer.

**Governing feasibility conclusion, unchanged and controlling.** The tested program does not support the land **at any price, including zero**. This decision changes a negotiation input; it does not change, soften, or displace that conclusion (CVR-50; R-21; Project Canon §3, CAN-020/021/022). Any use of the $7,500,000 figure that implies the acquisition has become supportable is prohibited.

**No transaction is authorized.** No offer submission, letter of intent, term sheet, rider, deposit, acquisition, or commitment of funds is authorized by this decision. No offer, LOI, or term sheet exists in the record (CVR-03; MA-18).

**Property status.** Prospective acquisition. The sponsor does not currently own the property. No document may imply ownership, control, contract, acquisition, or commitment without future evidence (CAN-003).

**Unresolved and expressly not decided here:** negotiating authority; maximum authorized purchase price; walk-away price; final offer conditions; transaction structure (cash, option, ground lease, participation, or contribution); and applicant or sponsor entity identity (OQ-33). OQ-20 remains open on all price limits and authority.

## Relationship to the modeled land basis — dual-basis discipline

**The modeled land basis is unchanged at $8,000,000.** S2 (`Assumptions!B18`), S3 (`Assumptions!D7`), the derived integrated development model, and every cost, yield, residual, and gap figure computed from them continue to carry an $8,000,000 land input. **No restatement at $7,500,000 has been performed or is authorized by this decision.**

Two bases therefore coexist and **must never be blended**:

| Basis | Value | Status | Where it applies |
|---|---|---|---|
| Working opening-offer basis | **$7,500,000** | Ratified nonbinding negotiation input (this decision) | Negotiation posture; contract drafting once authorized |
| Modeled land basis | **$8,000,000** | Unchanged model input | Every model output, cost table, yield, residual, and the canonical feasibility finding |

Restating the models at $7,500,000 is model-vNext work gated by [Document Update Order](../planning/Document%20Update%20Order.md) Step 5. It is not required for the feasibility conclusion, which holds at a land basis of zero: the $500,000 difference is immaterial against a shortfall that free land does not close.

**Rationale/evidence.** Owner ruling dated 2026-07-29. Context preserved rather than adopted: S8 p.7 records a buyer-side "defensible range" of $7.65M–$9.0M, placing $7,500,000 marginally below that stated floor; S6 concluded an appraisal opinion of $11,100,000 with documented internal inconsistencies (CVR-51, CVR-52, CVR-53); the prior listing was $12,000,000; the program-supported residual is approximately $0–$2M. None of these third-party ranges is an adopted limit.
