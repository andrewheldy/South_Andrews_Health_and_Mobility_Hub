# D-P9 — Governance succession

**Decision.** `AGENTS.md` and the current `docs/governance/` set are the operative repository governance system unless and until an authenticated prior Project Constitution or Decision Log is recovered and reconciled.

Recovered prior governance initially enters as historical evidence and does not automatically supersede current governance. The prior Constitution and Decision Log remain `missing_source_dependency`; recovery attempts continue.

**Affected:** all governance and planning references; MA-01 and MA-02; validation rules.

**Supersedes:** Statements describing absent prior governance as presently operative.

**Review trigger:** Authentication and recovery of a prior governance document.
