## 2. Proposed decision

Subject to §1 remaining unfilled, the following is the proposed operative text.

### 2.1 Conflicts check — authorized, limited

The sponsor is authorized to initiate a **limited written conflicts check** with TOOTHAKER.org. The request is confined to asking whether the firm or Ms. Toothaker holds any current or prior representation, retainer, or adverse relationship involving the parties enumerated in §3, and to obtaining the firm's written response.

The request may state that a prospective client is evaluating a mixed-use healthcare-anchored redevelopment of an identified Fort Lauderdale parcel. It may not characterize the transaction as pending, funded, approved, or agreed.

### 2.2 Disclosure limited to what conflicts screening requires

**Until written conflicts clearance is received, disclosure is limited to the identity-level items in §3.** No analysis, model output, register, workbook, valuation, strategy document, or internal assessment may be transmitted at the conflicts stage.

Following written clearance, disclosure is governed **item by item** by the [Controlled Disclosure Matrix](../partnerships/Controlled%20Disclosure%20Matrix.md), and every transmission carries the nine cover disclosures in [Counsel Meeting Materials Register](../partnerships/Counsel%20Meeting%20Materials%20Register.md) §5 and is entered in that document's transmission log.

**Ratifying this clause would close OQ-35** by determining that disclosure to a prospective, unengaged advisor under an express confidentiality and status cover is confidential professional-advisor disclosure rather than external publication — and therefore is not gated by [Document Update Order](../planning/Document%20Update%20Order.md) Step 8. The substantive claims rules continue to apply in full: accurate partner status, no implied approvals, and the current negative feasibility finding neither omitted nor obscured.

### 2.3 Privilege and sequencing caution

Substantive project material disclosed **before** an executed engagement letter may not attract attorney-client privilege. The proposed sequence therefore is: conflicts clearance → engagement letter or written consultation agreement → substantive disclosure → consultation. Where the owner elects to disclose substantive material at the consultation stage before an engagement letter is executed, the matrix marks those items accordingly and the privilege risk is accepted knowingly.

### 2.4 Initial paid strategy consultation — authorized in principle, gated on §1

The sponsor is authorized to commission **one bounded, paid initial strategy consultation**, contingent on (a) written conflicts clearance, (b) B-1 and B-2 being filled, and (c) a written scope confirmation. Scope is limited to the twelve items proposed in S9 §10 as re-grounded in [Land-Use Counsel Introductory Meeting Brief](../partnerships/Land-Use%20Counsel%20Introductory%20Meeting%20Brief.md) §6 — principally use characterization, the height pathway and review level, the accessory-parking ratio question, parking methodology, Live Local preemption, the approval sequence, and the feasibility and entitlement period structure.

### 2.6 Required deliverables

The consultation must produce, in writing:

1. A **Land-Use and Entitlement Strategy Memorandum** covering: project description as understood; applicable zoning framework against the actual ULDR text; proposed-use classification; approval pathway and review level; required applications; reviewing bodies; key risks; contract-contingency recommendations; estimated timeline; required consultants; recommended meeting sequence; and a go / modify / stop recommendation.
2. A written position on the **accessory-parking ratio limit** (OQ-27 / CVR-32) — the load-bearing entitlement argument, currently uncited in any source.
3. A written position on **shared-parking availability and parking methodology** (OQ-26 / CVR-31).
4. A written position on **Live Local Act preemption** of the 50 dwelling-units-per-net-acre cap (OQ-30 / CVR-53).
5. **Corrected duration ranges** for the feasibility and entitlement periods, tested against the analysis in [Meeting Brief](../partnerships/Land-Use%20Counsel%20Introductory%20Meeting%20Brief.md) §7.
6. A statement of what the firm would and would not undertake in a subsequent engagement, and whether governmental-relations work would trigger lobbying registration.

A verbal opinion does not satisfy any deliverable and does not close any gate: **G2 requires a written City determination and G3 requires a written counsel opinion.**

### 2.7 Status of counsel

**Stephanie J. Toothaker, Esq. remains prospective counsel until an engagement letter is executed.** Neither the conflicts check nor a paid consultation creates a retention, an ongoing relationship, or any authority to act for the sponsor. Consistent with D-P14, nothing she or her firm states becomes a repository fact: advice received is recorded as advice from a prospective or newly engaged advisor, and any answer contradicting a repository position is added to the [Contradiction and Validation Register](../planning/Contradiction%20and%20Validation%20Register.md) rather than overwriting that position.

Florida Bar standing and discipline history must be rechecked on the day of engagement (Advisor Profile V-1).
