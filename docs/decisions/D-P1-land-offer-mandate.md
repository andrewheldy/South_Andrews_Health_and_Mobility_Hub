# D-P1 — Land-offer mandate

> **Partial supersession, 2026-07-29.** The **$8,000,000 working opening-offer figure below is superseded by [D-P13](D-P13-acquisition-negotiation-basis.md)**, which sets the current nonbinding working opening-offer basis at **$7,500,000**. Every other provision of this record remains ratified and in force: the non-value and non-authority characterization, the property status, the required diligence conditions, and the unresolved authority items. The $8,000,000 figure is preserved here as a **superseded assumption** (D-P8) and remains the **unchanged modeled land basis** in S2, S3, and the derived model — see D-P13's dual-basis rule. Content below is otherwise untouched.

**Decision.** The working opening offer is **$8,000,000**. It is an acquisition-strategy input, not proof of value and not, by itself, authority to submit an offer.

**Unresolved:** negotiating authority; maximum authorized purchase price; walk-away price; final offer conditions.

**Property status:** Prospective acquisition. The sponsor does not currently own the property. No document may imply ownership, control, contract, acquisition, or commitment without future evidence.

**Required diligence conditions:** physical and architectural test-fit; entitlement and zoning verification; environmental review; survey, title, and legal review; utility and FPL capacity review; flood and stormwater feasibility; rent and market validation; revised development feasibility; financing and partner support.

**Rationale/evidence:** Owner instruction dated 2026-07-26; S2 `Assumptions!B18`; S3 `Assumptions!D7`; S8 p.7. The sources support the figure as a planning/strategy input only.

**Affected:** canon; acquisition assumptions; manifest; models when later authorized; external-use controls.

**Supersedes:** Any implication that $8,000,000 is proven value, a price ceiling, a walk-away price, or present transaction authority.

**Review trigger:** New diligence, written transaction authority, or a later owner price decision.
