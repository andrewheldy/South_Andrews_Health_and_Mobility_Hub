# D-P14 — Advisor research source classification

**Decision.** `sources/people/Stephanie_J_Toothaker_Context.md` (**S9**) is admitted as a **provisional external-advisor research source**.

**Permitted uses.** S9 may support:

1. advisor evaluation;
2. conflicts preparation;
3. meeting preparation;
4. proposed-scope drafting.

**Authority.** S9 carries **no authority** for site, zoning, legal, economic, contract, approval, or stakeholder claims. It may not be cited as support for any such claim, in any document, at any classification. Project statements appearing inside S9 — including its address form, its site-area figure, its program description, its characterization of the feasibility position, its named sponsor entity, and its acquisition figure — derive no authority from this admission and remain governed by the ratified record and by the higher-authority sources in [Source Authority Register](../planning/Source%20Authority%20Register.md).

**Advisor status.** Stephanie J. Toothaker, Esq. remains a **prospective advisor only**. No contact, conflicts clearance, engagement letter, scope agreement, fee arrangement, or legal opinion exists. Nothing in S9 or in this decision establishes availability, interest, absence of conflict, professional fit, or any retention.

**Classification recorded.** Provisional; `source_provenance: owner_provided`; `verification_status: unverified`; `external_use_status: internal_only`; **no evidentiary authority tier**. Its underlying public sources are not captured in the repository and remain a tracked dependency (MA-19). Florida Bar standing and discipline history must be rechecked immediately before any engagement.

**Rationale/evidence.** Owner ruling dated 2026-07-29. The file is an owner-provided synthesis of publicly available professional information about a prospective advisor. It is competent to inform how the sponsor prepares to evaluate and meet that advisor; it is not evidence about the property, its entitlement, its economics, or any third party's position. The distinction follows the treatment already applied to the ungoverned electrical load model (CVR-01 / OQ-29): admission and use are separable from authority.

**Affected:** [Source Inventory](../planning/Source%20Inventory.md) S9 entry; [Decision Log](../planning/Decision%20Log.md); [Open Questions](../planning/Open%20Questions.md) OQ-34; the counsel package in [`docs/partnerships/`](../partnerships/README.md); structured exports (source register).

**Resolves:** the **authority-classification portion of OQ-34** only.

**Does not resolve:**

- **OQ-36** — whether `sources/people/` is a recognized repository source class, and where advisor, stakeholder, and counterparty context files belong. Preserved as a separate folder-structure question.
- **OQ-35** — whether transmission of project material to a prospective, unengaged advisor is external use under the claims policy or confidential advisor disclosure.
- **D-P15 (pending)** — counsel engagement authority, scope, and fee basis, and the disclosure determination.

**Supersedes:** nothing. This is a first classification, not a replacement.

**Review trigger:** An owner ruling on the `sources/people/` class; engagement of counsel; recovery of the primary-source captures (MA-19); or any proposal to cite S9 beyond the four permitted uses.
