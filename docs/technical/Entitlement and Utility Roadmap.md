**Working position for planning:** both the 6- and 8-storey scenarios assume **discretionary review above six storeys**; the exact envelope, review level, FAR, and setbacks are **unverified pending a Zoning Verification Letter and pre-application meeting**. A scheme at four storeys or fewer avoids the height-review trigger entirely — which is the principal entitlement argument for SA-C.

## 3. Critical path

The binding sequence is **not** the building permit. It is:

**Recover Phase I/II ESA (1.3) → land-use counsel opinion (1.5) → Zoning Verification Letter (1.6) → pre-application (1.7) → test-fit (1.10) → FPL load letter (1.9) → GC pricing (1.13) → acquisition decision.**

Every one of these can be completed **before** acquisition, and together they cost roughly **$100–200K**. That is the correct scale of spend before committing $8M of land and $30–70M of construction. Two of them — the ESA recovery and the FPL load letter — could each independently change the acquisition decision.

**FPL is the schedule risk.** Transformer and switchgear lead times have run beyond 12 months since 2022. The FPL application must be filed at schematic design.

## 4. Entitlement risk by scenario

| Scenario | Height review | Accessory-parking ratio | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SA-A** (8 storeys, 385 stalls) | **Discretionary review required** | **1.66×** | Highest risk. Discretionary height plus a parking-heavy program with an uncited accessory argument, plus medium-voltage service |
| **SA-B** (6 storeys, 231 stalls) | At the threshold — six storeys is where review begins. **The exact trigger is unverified** | **1.00×** | Moderate. Parks itself exactly; no surplus. Ironically the *safest* accessory argument and the *weakest* parking economics — the same fact drives both |
| **SA-C** (4 storeys, 169 stalls) | **No height review trigger** | **1.17×** | **Lowest risk.** Below the review threshold, modest surplus, by-right uses, active frontage satisfied |
| **SA-C0** (1 storey, 70 stalls) | **No height review** | **1.25×** | Minimal. Essentially a conventional small commercial site plan |
| *S8 Scenario A (9 storeys, ~575 stalls)* | *Discretionary* | ***4.3×*** | *Rejected program. The accessory argument at this ratio is the weakest of any option* |

**The entitlement pathway rewards restraint precisely where the economics do too.** Both the approval risk and the capital risk fall as the scheme gets smaller.

## 5. What must not be claimed

Not supported by anything in the repository, and must not appear in any external document:

- That 12 storeys, 110 ft, or 150 ft is permitted or available by right on this site
- That the review is "Level III" (S8 asserts it; no ULDR text is quoted anywhere)
- That no rezoning is required (probable, but it is a City determination, not ours)
- That FAR is unlimited
- That surplus parking qualifies as accessory at any particular ratio
- That FPL capacity has been confirmed, or that any service size is available
- That the City supports, endorses, or has reviewed this project
- That the site is environmentally clean
- That any partner, operator, or health system is involved

Approved language: *proposed · prospective · planning-level · subject to City review · subject to utility confirmation · subject to test fit · not yet validated.*
