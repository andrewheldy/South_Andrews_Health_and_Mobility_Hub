# Institutional Prospectus

Read the full governance set and complete source reconciliation and financial-model audit before drafting. Stop if a material unresolved conflict would change the thesis, program, cost, status, or required disclosure.

## Audience

Write for:

- City officials
- healthcare systems
- investors
- lenders
- development partners
- mobility and infrastructure partners

Keep the core narrative accessible across audiences and move audience-specific technical detail to appendices.

## Procedure

1. Establish the strongest defensible thesis from controlling current evidence.
2. State clearly what the project is, its healthcare anchor, and how mobility and infrastructure enable it.
3. Distinguish vision, proposal, working assumptions, model outputs, verified facts, recommendations, and unresolved questions.
4. Cite every material fact to its original source and apply the claim record required by `docs/governance/CLAIMS_AND_EVIDENCE_POLICY.md`.
5. Write concise, concrete prose. Remove hype, generic AI language, unsupported superlatives, and implied certainty.
6. Treat organizations as prospective or potential partners unless documented commitment supports stronger language.
7. Include clear acquisition, entitlement, design, partnership, utility, and cost status disclosures.
8. Put detailed model methodology, assumptions, source notes, diligence items, and technical support in appendices.
9. Label every rendering conspicuously as **Illustrative rendering** and explain that it is subject to diligence, test fit, design development, City review, and other applicable approvals.
10. Review headlines, tables, captions, logos, images, and layout for implied endorsement or unsupported commitment.
11. Run a final claim-to-source and cross-document consistency check before external use.

## Non-negotiable constraints

- Do not imply ownership; state that the property is a prospective acquisition and the sponsor does not currently own it.
- Do not imply City, healthcare-system, investor, lender, developer, mobility-provider, or utility endorsement.
- Do not present planning-level estimates as finalized costs.
- Do not present model output as a verified fact or guaranteed result.
- Do not depend on speculative AV revenue.
- Do not allow a polished legacy narrative to override current evidence.

## Quality test

A skeptical reviewer should understand:

- what the project is
- why the site matters
- what is proposed
- what is modeled
- what remains uncertain
- what must happen next

If any answer depends on implication, promotional language, or an uncited material claim, revise the prospectus before external use.
