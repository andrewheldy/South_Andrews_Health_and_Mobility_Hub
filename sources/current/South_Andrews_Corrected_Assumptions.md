# South Andrews Healthcare & Mobility Hub

## Corrected Assumptions & Investment Thesis (Grounded Review)

> This document separates **Verified Facts**, **Appraisal Opinions**,
> and **Project Hypotheses** so future modeling and partner materials
> remain internally consistent.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Executive Summary

The September 2025 appraisal supports a fee-simple land value of
**\$11.1 million** using a sales comparison approach. That appraisal
should **not** be interpreted as proof the property is worth \$11.1M to
every buyer, nor as validation of the proposed Healthcare & Mobility
Hub.

Our current investment thesis is stronger when it: - Uses the appraisal
for factual site characteristics. - Challenges the appraisal where its
comparable selection introduces upward bias. - Clearly labels future
development assumptions as hypotheses until verified.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. VERIFIED FACTS

## Property

-   Address assemblage commonly referenced as **901--917 S Andrews
    Avenue**.
-   Land area: **38,207 SF (0.88 acres)**.
-   Corner parcel with approximately **275 ft** on S Andrews and **170
    ft** on SW 9th.
-   FEMA Zone AE.
-   RAC-RPO zoning.
-   Existing improvements are three older commercial buildings.
-   Hardy Park lies immediately north.

## Appraisal

-   Effective date: **September 18, 2025**
-   Report date: **September 25, 2025**
-   Interest appraised: **Fee Simple**
-   Value conclusion: **\$11,100,000**
-   Exposure time: \~12 months
-   Marketing time: \~12 months

## Highest & Best Use

The appraiser concludes redevelopment into a mixed-use project is the
highest and best use.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. WHAT THE APPRAISAL DOES NOT PROVE

It does **not** establish:

-   demand for medical office
-   demand for imaging
-   AV fleet demand
-   EV charging demand
-   utility capacity for high electrical loads
-   hospital partnership interest
-   parking demand
-   feasibility of the Mobility Hub

These require separate diligence.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3. IMPORTANT APPRAISAL LIMITATIONS

The appraisal explicitly rejected: - owner operating statements - owner
NOI - related-party leases

These were not considered reliable market evidence.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. WHY THE \$11.1M VALUE CAN BE CHALLENGED

The valuation relied on four land sales.

Important observations:

-   One sale involved a condemnation negotiation.
-   One sale was purchased as part of Broward Health assemblage.
-   Two superior downtown sites enjoyed stronger entitlements.
-   The adjusted value range remained broad.

Therefore the reconciliation required substantial judgment.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 5. VERIFIED DEVELOPMENT CONSTRAINTS

Verified:

-   Above six stories requires enhanced review.
-   Up to roughly twelve stories may be possible under applicable
    RAC-RPO provisions subject to review.
-   Flood design must comply with Zone AE.
-   Parking, frontage, and mixed-use requirements remain important.

Not yet verified:

-   exact maximum FAR
-   final height pathway
-   TDR opportunities
-   Live Local applicability
-   Opportunity Zone strategy

Verified:

-   Downtown proximity
-   Airport proximity
-   Hospital corridor
-   Large assembled parcel

-   Strong redevelopment location

# 7. RISKS

Verified:

-   Flood Zone AE
-   Higher insurance costs
-   Entitlement review above six stories
-   Long marketing period

Project risks:

-   Healthcare leasing
-   Utility upgrades
-   FPL capacity
-   Construction pricing
-   Capital markets

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 8. INVESTMENT POSITION

The appraisal supports that the site has substantial redevelopment
value.

It does **not** invalidate an approximately **\$8M opening offer**,
because:

-   appraisal is lender-oriented
-   buyer-specific risk differs
-   entitlement risk remains
-   construction costs are significant
-   infrastructure investment is substantial
