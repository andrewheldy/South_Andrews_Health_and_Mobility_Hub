## 9. Relevance to the South Andrews Project

### Project Context

Heldy’s LLC is evaluating the acquisition and redevelopment of approximately 0.88 acres at 901–915 South Andrews Avenue in Fort Lauderdale.

The emerging strategy is:

1. **South Andrews urban interface**
   - Healthcare-oriented mixed-use development
   - Medical retail and possible ambulatory or clinical uses
   - Patient pickup and drop-off
   - Structured parking
   - Limited vehicle staging
   - EV charging
   - Rental and mobility functions
   - Future autonomous-vehicle optionality

2. **Separate industrial depot**
   - Fleet storage
   - Large-scale charging
   - Cleaning
   - Servicing
   - Operational scale

The development economics do not currently support Heldy’s LLC self-financing both the vertical healthcare project and the fleet depot. The preferred direction is therefore partner-first: an experienced healthcare developer or institutional partner would carry the vertical-development burden while Heldy’s focuses on mobility operations and infrastructure.

### Why Ms. Toothaker May Be Relevant

Her public profile appears directly relevant to the following South Andrews needs:

- Interpreting the RAC-RPO zoning framework
- Determining whether the proposed use mix is legally supportable
- Structuring rental, patient-transfer, mobility-staging, parking, and charging uses as principal or accessory uses
- Identifying whether rezoning, conditional use, site-plan approval, or other relief is required
- Advising on the correct pre-application and DRC strategy
- Designing a realistic entitlement timeline
- Reviewing the purchase-contract entitlement contingency
- Defining the site-plan approval milestone required before closing
- Coordinating legal strategy with planning, architecture, traffic, engineering, utilities, and environmental consultants
- Identifying political, neighborhood, design, traffic, and public-realm concerns
- Advising how and when to engage Broward Health, FLL, Port Everglades, Broward County, FDOT, FPL, and City stakeholders
- Helping distinguish true governmental approvals from commercial partnerships or operating permissions
- Evaluating whether a public-private, healthcare-access, patient-mobility, or innovation narrative adds legitimate value
- Protecting future AV optionality without allowing speculative AV language to undermine current approval prospects

## 10. Recommended Initial Assignment

The first engagement should be narrow and decision-oriented rather than an open-ended entitlement mandate.

### Proposed Phase 1 Scope

Request a paid strategic review covering:

1. Review of the current development concept
2. Review of RAC-RPO use and dimensional constraints
3. Preliminary determination of the likely approval pathway
4. Identification of fatal or material entitlement risks
5. Assessment of whether rental and limited mobility staging can be accessory uses
6. Review of the proposed purchase-contract diligence and entitlement structure
7. Recommendation on whether closing should require final site-plan approval, DRC approval, a defined intermediate milestone, or another governmental determination
8. Recommended pre-application strategy
9. Identification of the minimum consultant team
10. Recommended entitlement schedule and budget range
11. Stakeholder and governmental-relations strategy
12. Written next-step memorandum

A concise written **Land-Use and Entitlement Strategy Memorandum** containing:

- Project description

- Approval pathway
- Required applications
- Reviewing bodies
- Key risks
- Contract-contingency recommendations
- Estimated timeline
- Required consultants
- Recommended meeting sequence
- Go / modify / stop recommendation

## 11. Questions for the First Meeting

### Use and Zoning

1. Can the current concept be approved under RAC-RPO without rezoning or a land-use-plan amendment?
2. How should the project’s primary use be described?
3. Can limited rental-vehicle staging, passenger transfer, fleet turnover, EV charging, and mobility operations qualify as accessory uses?
4. What uses or operational details are most likely to create objections?
5. Should autonomous-vehicle language be excluded from the initial application?

### Approval Pathway

6. What formal approval path is likely?
7. Which bodies will review or approve the project?
8. Is a pre-application meeting sufficient for initial diligence?
9. What is the realistic timeline from application preparation through final approval?
10. What approval milestone should be required before closing?

### Contract Protection

11. Is a 75-day initial feasibility period appropriate?
12. Is a 240-day entitlement period with two 60-day extensions realistic?
13. Should the deposit remain refundable until an acceptable entitlement milestone is reached?
14. How should “acceptable site-plan approval” be defined?
15. What owner authorizations and cooperation covenants must the seller provide?
16. What happens if the City approves the plan with economically unacceptable conditions?
17. Should partner formation, healthcare tenancy, utility capacity, or financing remain express contingencies?

### Governmental and Stakeholder Strategy

18. Which City staff should be engaged first?
19. At what point should elected officials or neighborhood stakeholders be briefed?
20. Is there value in seeking a letter of support from Broward Health or another healthcare institution?
21. Which airport, port, County, FDOT, or utility interactions are necessary during diligence?
22. Does the project have a credible public-benefit or public-private positioning?
23. What should not be said publicly at this stage?

### Team and Budget

24. Which consultants should be retained before the pre-application meeting?
25. Which consultants should wait until after the City gives preliminary feedback?
26. What is a realistic pre-closing entitlement budget?
27. Who should serve as applicant and development sponsor if a healthcare-development partner has not yet been selected?
28. Can her firm assist in identifying or evaluating appropriate development partners?

## 13. Desired Meeting Outcome

The meeting should produce clarity on five decisions:

1. **Is the concept legally and politically viable enough to place the property under contract?**
2. **What exact governmental milestone should protect the buyer before closing?**
3. **How long should the diligence and entitlement periods be?**
4. **How should the proposed mobility functions be structured and described?**
5. **What team, budget, and sequence are required to reach a reliable go/no-go decision?**

The purpose is not to ask Ms. Toothaker to validate the vision. The purpose is to obtain a candid, experienced assessment of whether the concept can be approved, how it must change, what it will cost to pursue, and how Heldy’s LLC should protect itself contractually while doing so.
